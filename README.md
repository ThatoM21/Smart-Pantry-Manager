<h1> Smart Pantry Manager </h1><br>

Android Application that is designed to eliminate food waste, and maximize every grocery item. <br>

<h2> About the Project </h2><br>

The Smart Pantry Manager is a native Java Android application that addresses a critical challenge faced by many university and college students: managing limited food supplies effectively without wasting scarce financial resource. <br>

By tracking the existing household ingredients and enforcing a strict recipe-matching algorithm, the app ensures that students cook meals using only what they already have at home preventing unnecessary trips to the grocery store and protecting tight monthly budgets. <br>

<h2> The Student Financial Context </h2><br>

For thousands of university students funded by financial aid schemes and bursaries, financial planning is a delicate balancing act. By understanding the economic realities of the student life, its able to highlight why a tool like Smart Pantry Manager is essential: <br>

<ul>
  <li> Schemes such as NSFAS have a monthly meal allowance between R1600 and R1700, this leaves very little room for error. With rising food inflation, buying duplicate ingredients, letting fresh produce spoil or impulse buying-takeouts because "there's nothing to eat" can exhaust a student's food budget by week 3 of the month.
  </li>
</ul><br>

<h3> How the app protects students wallets</h3><br>
<ul>
